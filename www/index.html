About

What is IW4x

IW4x is a multiplayer client for Call of Duty: Modern Warfare 2 (2009).

Two versions are available, each built around a different way to play.

x86
is built around community-hosted dedicated servers. Each server can have its own rules and mods.
x64
is built around matchmaking, with friends and parties available directly in the client.

Support

Frequently asked questions

Is IW4x a mod?

IW4x is a multiplayer client for Modern Warfare 2. Mods are supported on both x86 and x64, but are entirely optional. You can install IW4x and play without touching them.

Which client should I use?

That mostly comes down to how you want to play. x86 is centred around community-hosted dedicated servers. x64 uses matchmaking, with a friends list and parties for playing together.

Can I play with friends?

You can play together on either client. x64 lets you add friends and invite them directly to a party. On x86, you can join the same server or host one for your friends to join.
